## Navigation and maintenance

- [Start at the tree](../INDEX.md), choose a category, then a branch, then a `SKILL.md`.
- Navigation is generated by `scripts/sync_catalog.py`; do not edit those pages manually.
- `scripts/migrate_category_tree.py` is read-only by default; `--apply` requires an explicit request. It checks collisions and rolls back package/metadata changes on failure. On the current checkout it plans zero moves.
- Current catalog and README links were updated. Historical source reports, assignment ledgers, and previous audit counts remain snapshots of their respective dates.
- Old batch generators that assume a nine-category layout or flat index are not current maintenance commands. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
